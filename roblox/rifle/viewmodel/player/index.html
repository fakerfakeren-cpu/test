Scar viewmodel animations

Every frame is a keyframe from ScarViewmodel.rbxmx, framed the way your game frames it. The ♪ tags on the timeline are the sound events your template listens for; they light up when the playhead passes them.

    How these were made

    Built from your place file: your AutoBlaster viewmodel's parts, your camera offset, and your tool's settings (reloadTime 1.5 s, rateOfFire 600). Each keyframe was replayed through the model's joints the way Roblox's Animator does, then drawn from two cameras.

    Your template plays sounds from animation events. An event named Sound plays the AudioPlayer of that name from the tool's Sounds folder, and RandomSound plays a random one from the named folder. This page marks those events but can't play your audio, which lives on Roblox.

    The arms are drawn as their 0.6 × 0.6 × 4 parts. In game your view model controller hides those parts and shows its sleeve mesh in the same place, so the arms will look different from these boxes.

    Keyboard: space plays or pauses, the arrow keys step one frame, 1 to 4 switch clips.
