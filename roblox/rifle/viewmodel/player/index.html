    How these were made

    The setup script was run with the real Luau interpreter against a mock of your viewmodel rebuilt from ScarView.gltf, and these frames come from the KeyframeSequences it produced. Each Pose was applied to its Motor6D the way Roblox's Animator does. The gun and magazine are your exported meshes; the arms are the template's 0.6 × 0.6 × 4 stud blocks.

    Keyboard: space plays or pauses, the arrow keys step one frame, 1 to 4 switch clips.
