# Mimas (Sofia) — Minecraft Java 26.3 data pack

A block-by-block Minecraft version of **Mimas**, the döner / falafel takeaway at Graf Ignatiev / Vasil Levski in
central Sofia. It's a generic Sofia-centre interpretation, built without photos: the ground floor of an older
5-storey residential block, the lit **МИМАС** sign band, tram street outside. It is not a replica of the real
façade.

**File:** [`mimas-datapack.zip`](mimas-datapack.zip). Vanilla only, Creative, one command.

## Install (Java 26.3)
1. Put `mimas-datapack.zip` in `<world>/datapacks/` (or *Create World → Data Packs → drag it in*).
2. In the world: `/reload` (cheats on).
3. Stand on flat ground where the **sidewalk** should be, then `/function mimas:build`.
4. Undo: `/function mimas:clear` (puts the original terrain back in the same session).

## Where it appears
- You are the origin. You end up on the sidewalk in front of the shop, turned to face it (north).
- Footprint **26 × 26 blocks, 34 high**: from 10 blocks west to 15 east, 13 north to 12 south, 2 below your feet
  to 31 above. Everything in that box is replaced (cleared to air first).
- North of you: the shop (21 × 11, interior 19 × 9, ceiling 5 high) plus the flats' entrance bay. South of you:
  the sidewalk, curb, road with two tram tracks, and the far sidewalk.
