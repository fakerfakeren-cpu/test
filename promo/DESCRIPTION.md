# Oathbound: The Hollow Crown

*The Order of the Lantern swore to keep the dark at bay. One oath broke, and the Order fell with it.
You are the last squire, and the oaths are yours to keep.*

**Oathbound** is a story-driven adventure mod: a hand-built questline across six chapters, puzzle keeps
guarded by sleeping bosses, a whole new dimension, and a world that remembers the Order that once walked it.
It's a single jar with no dependencies.

---

## ✦ The Path

Your **Lantern Chronicle** (given on first join) guides you through six chapters:

1. **The Last Squire.** Mine lumenite, forge oathsteel, craft the Warden's Lantern and kindle a roadside Wayshrine.
2. **Where the Bells Drown.** In the Drowned Chapel, ring the bells in the hymn's order to raise the crypt grate, then face **Sir Caldris, the Drowned Knight**.
3. **The Hollow Spire.** Climb the Arcanist's Spire, answer the Archmage's riddles on the rune dials, then face **Archmage Veyl** and his mirror images.
4. **The Barrow of Kings.** Read the three epitaphs, kneel at the one honest king's tomb, then face **Hrodgar, the Barrow-King**, and his housecarls.
5. **The Sundered Gate.** Bind the three seals into the **Oathkey** and perform the rite that tears the Gate open.
6. **The Hollow Crown.** Cross into **the Gloaming**, climb to the Hollow Throne and break **Morvane, the Hollow King**, in a three-phase fight in a darkening arena.

Your lantern's *Seek* points the way to the next keep, so you are never lost.

## ✦ Bosses worth preparing for

- **Seven bosses** with their own arenas, attacks, music and spellwork: three seal keepers, three wild keepers and the Hollow King.
- **Sealed keeps.** Each keeper sleeps behind its puzzle. You can't dig around the wards, and the keeper won't wake until the puzzle is solved.
- **Fights that matter.** If everyone falls or flees, the boss returns to its vigil at full health and you face it again from the start.
- **Built for friends.** Bosses grow stronger with every extra challenger.
- **The Chronicle's "Prepare" notes** tell you what armour, weapons and elixirs to bring to each fight.

## ✦ The Lantern Chronicle

A hand-illustrated quest book, not a wall of text:

- **The Path:** every chapter drawn as a branching path of seals. Each quest has a picture of its goal (the place itself, the creature, or the recipe you need) and clear directions.
- **Tithes:** quest rewards, collected from the book or at any lit Wayshrine, where they rise out of the fire.
- **18 Oath Boons:** finish a chapter and swear one of three permanent boons.
- **Bestiary and Armory:** entries unlock as you meet each creature and hold each relic, with tips on how to fight and use them.

## ✦ The Gloaming

A dimension of endless dusk, with three biomes: the grey Gloaming, the violet **Veilwood** and the burnt **Ashen Reach**.
Gloamrot creeps in unless your lantern stays lit. Shade wraiths nest in a shattered observatory, and the Hollow Throne waits on the high island.
Once you have opened the Gate, your **Oathkey** carries you there and back from anywhere.

## ✦ Off the Path

The story is only half the world.

- **Three wild keepers**, each in its own lair: the **Elderhorn**, the stag-king of the forests; the **Bog Mother** in her swamp stilt-house; and the **Cinder Colossus** in a magma hall under a desert pyramid.
- **Places to explore:** Lanternguard watchtowers, a crystal sea grotto, an abandoned lumenite mine, waystones on forgotten roads, ruined outposts and glimmer glades.
- **Two dozen creatures:** glimmerfawns, mossback tortoises, thornback boars, stonewardens, runewisps, mire hags, veilhounds, Forsworn knights who rise again unless you finish them with fire or light, and more.
- **A rideable mount:** tame and saddle a **Glimmerstag**.
- **The Lanternguard Pilgrim:** a wandering trader of the Order, often drawn to a kindled Wayshrine.

## ✦ A world that feels alive

Fireflies over summer grass, blossoms drifting through cherry groves, plankton glowing on night water, spores over the swamps, and motes rising from lumenite in the caves.
Birdsong by day; crickets, owls and frogs by night; wind on the heights. **Every sound in the mod was made for it.**

## ✦ Gear, magic and crafting

- **Oathsteel** arms and armour, plus **five more metals** (tidebronze, runesilver, gravegold, duskiron, dawnsteel), each with its own tools, armour, set bonus and signature weapon.
- **Relics** with active powers, **4 new enchantments**, **6 elixirs** and hearty food.
- **Five stone building families**, lanterns and decor, **7 paintings** and **6 music discs**.
- Every recipe a quest asks for is shown right in the Chronicle.
